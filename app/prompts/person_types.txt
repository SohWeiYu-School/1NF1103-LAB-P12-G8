# prompt_version: person-types-v1
# Call 3, step 1: every kind of person this client could be.

TASK: List every kind of person this client could possibly be from {start_year} to
{last_forecast_year}.

- There is NO fixed list. Build the types from THIS client's profile: his jobs, industries,
  employers, countries, assets, claimed sources and declaration.
- Cover the full range his profile allows: the version where his wealth came exactly as
  declared, versions with tax or disclosure problems, and every kind of financial crime he
  could realistically be involved in. Include a type only if his profile gives it a real
  route (access, means and opportunity).
- type_id "{baseline_type}" MUST be the version where his wealth came exactly as declared.
  Then "T2", "T3", ... Each type must be a different route.
- route: the specific route in one sentence (e.g. "over-invoices freight through his Hong
  Kong brokerage", not "money launderer").
- rule_out_documents: documents that would show this type is NOT the true one.
  also_rules_out may ONLY contain type ids from your list ([] if none).
- Do not rank the types or say which is more likely.
