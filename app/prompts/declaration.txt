# prompt_version: declaration-v1
# Step 0: turn the client's free-text declaration into a list of claimed sources.

TASK: Read the Source of Wealth declaration and list every source of money it claims.

- claimed_sources: one entry per source, in the order they appear. source_id "S1", "S2", ...
- source_type: one of the source_types in the vocabulary.
- description: the source in a few plain words.
- start_year / end_year: the years the declaration gives for it, or null.
- amount: only an amount the declaration actually states, in {currency}; otherwise null.
  Do not estimate amounts.
- country: the country the money came from, as a full country name.
- linked_position_index: the position_index in career_history this source comes from, or null.
- linked_asset_index: the asset_index in assets this source comes from or paid for, or null.
- quote: the exact words of the declaration you took it from.
Include sources that are only mentioned in passing.
