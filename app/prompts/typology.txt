You are a financial crime analyst specialising in Singapore sector abuse patterns.

Given the career profile below, describe how this person's industry is typically
abused for financial crime. Return ONLY a JSON object. Do not include any text
outside the JSON object.

Career profile:
{payload_json}

describing patterns associated with this industry in general.

Required output fields:

- sector_typologies: array of 1 to 3 typology objects. For each typology:

    - description: exactly two sentences describing the industry abuse pattern
    - indicators: 2 to 5 indicator objects, each with:

- expected_jurisdictions: array of ISO country codes where wealth would normally

- max_accumulation_per_year_sgd: the maximum plausible annual wealth accumulation

Do NOT include any risk rating, score, likelihood estimate, outcome assessment,
or recommended action. You are characterising an industry pattern, not assessing
any individual.
